The [Radiant Saunas Coronado 2-Person](amazon:radiant-coronado-2-person) (by Blue Wave) earns its spot in this guide on one decisive attribute: a 7-year limited warranty, the longest coverage of any cabin we cover. It's a 49"W x 39"D x 75"H hemlock cabin with 6 carbon heaters, chromotherapy, and an oxygen ionizer, typically priced ~$1,499–$1,999. If you plan to own your sauna for a decade and want the most coverage behind it, the Coronado is the pick — but check the current price on Amazon before buying.

## Key specs

- Exterior dimensions: 49"W x 39"D x 75"H
- 6 carbon far-infrared heaters
- Standard 120V/15A plug-and-play
- Natural Canadian hemlock
- Chromotherapy + oxygen ionizer
- 7-year limited warranty (longest in this guide)

## Who it's for

Long-horizon buyers. The Coronado suits people who buy appliances once and keep them — the ones who'd rather pay a little more upfront for seven years of coverage than gamble on a cheaper cabin with a shorter warranty. It's also a roomy two-person cabin at 49 inches wide, so it works for couples who want genuine space rather than a snug squeeze.

## What stands out

**The warranty.** Seven years limited is the standout figure in this guide. We compared specs across all ten models, and nothing else reaches it — Dynamic offers 5, JNH 2, SunRay 7 structural only. For an appliance with electrical heaters you use in a hot, humid environment, warranty length is a meaningful proxy for the manufacturer's confidence.

**Size.** At 49"W x 39"D x 75"H, this is one of the roomiest 2-person cabins here — a step up from the Venice Elite's 43 inches. Couples get real shoulder room.

**Extras.** Chromotherapy and an oxygen ionizer come standard. The ionizer is a differentiator few competitors include; owner reports vary on how much difference it makes, but it's a genuine inclusion rather than a marketing badge. The chromotherapy lighting adds a customizable ambiance that owners of mood-lit cabins tend to appreciate more than they expected.

**Build.** Canadian hemlock construction with 6 carbon heaters is the proven formula for this tier — even heat, manageable weight, easy maintenance. Six heaters across a 49-inch cabin means solid coverage with no obvious cold zones, and the 120V/15A setup keeps installation at zero.

## Considerations

The 120V/15A plug-and-play setup is standard, so no electrical advantages or surprises — which is fine, since simplicity is part of the appeal. Heat-up behavior is comparable to other cabins this size; the larger interior takes a bit longer than compact models, so plan a slightly longer preheat in cold rooms. And the price sits mid-pack at ~$1,499–$1,999 — you're paying a modest premium for the warranty and size, which is exactly the trade-off to weigh against the cheaper JNH Joyous. If the Joyous's 2-year warranty doesn't bother you, it's the value alternative; if it does, the Coronado's 7 years are worth the difference.

## Verdict

The Radiant Saunas Coronado 2-Person is the warranty pick: the longest coverage in this guide, a genuinely roomy two-person cabin, and a solid feature set with chromotherapy and an oxygen ionizer. If "will this last" is your top question, seven years of coverage is the strongest answer under $2,000. Check the current price on Amazon to see where it sits in its range.
