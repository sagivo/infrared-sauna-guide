The [HeatWave Santa Fe 3-Person Corner](amazon:heatwave-santa-fe-3-person) is the only cabin in this guide that fits three people and tucks into a room corner — a 53–55"W x 53–55"D x 75"H unit with 7 carbon heaters and a 7-year warranty. The catch: it needs a 120V/20A circuit, making it the one model here that isn't simple plug-and-play. Street pricing runs ~$1,799–$2,499, so check the current price on Amazon.

## Key specs

- Exterior dimensions: 53–55"W x 53–55"D x 75"H (corner design)
- 7 carbon far-infrared heaters
- 1805W — 120V/20A required (not standard 15A)
- Natural Canadian hemlock
- Chromotherapy included
- 7-year warranty
- True 3-person capacity

## Who it's for

Families and social sauna users. The Santa Fe suits households where two or three people will sauna together regularly — or where one person wants lounge-grade room. The corner design is specifically for rooms where a rectangular cabin wastes wall space; sliding into a corner can be the difference between fitting and not fitting in a finished basement or large bathroom.

## What stands out

**Capacity.** Three-person infrared cabins under $2,500 are rare on Amazon. We compared specs across the guide, and the Santa Fe shares the top capacity slot with the Dynamic Madrid — but at a lower typical price and with a space-saving corner footprint.

**Power.** 1805W is the highest wattage in this guide, which generally translates to faster heat-up for a larger cabin — a real advantage when you're warming three people's worth of interior volume. The trade-off is the 20A circuit requirement — plan for an electrician visit if your panel doesn't already have a 20A line where you want the sauna. See our [electrical guide](/posts/infrared-sauna-electrical-requirements/) for what that involves and how it affects total cost versus 15A models.

**Heaters and features.** Seven carbon heaters provide full coverage, and chromotherapy comes standard. The 7-year warranty matches the longest coverage here.

**Build.** Canadian hemlock keeps the cabin light enough to assemble in a corner without a crew, and dual considerations for corner placement (door swing, panel access) are handled in the design.

## Considerations

The 20A requirement is the big one. Most homes have 20A circuits in kitchens and garages but not in bedrooms or basements where saunas usually go. An electrician adding a dedicated 20A line is a real cost — factor it into the total budget, and see our electrical requirements guide for what that typically involves.

Size is the other: 53–55 inches square is a large footprint even in a corner. Measure carefully, including door swing and assembly clearance — corner units need working room on two sides during the build.

## Verdict

The HeatWave Santa Fe 3-Person Corner is the capacity pick for households that sauna together: three-person room, corner-saving footprint, 7 carbon heaters, 1805W of heating power, and a 7-year warranty. Just go in knowing it needs a 20A circuit — that's the price of admission. Check the current price on Amazon and confirm your electrical situation before ordering.

## Worth knowing before you buy

**The 20A circuit is the real price.** The Santa Fe is the only model here that needs 120V/20A, and that requirement decides the purchase for many buyers. Check your breaker panel first: garages and laundry rooms often already have 20A lines; bedrooms and basements rarely do. If you need an electrician, get the quote before comparing prices — the work can narrow or erase the gap against 15A three-person cabins like the [Dynamic Madrid](/saunas/dynamic-madrid-3-person/). Our [electrical requirements guide](/posts/infrared-sauna-electrical-requirements/) has the full 15A vs 20A picture.

**Corner placement saves real space.** The 53–55 inch square footprint sounds large, but tucked into a corner it consumes less usable room than a rectangular 3-person cabin. Measure the corner, the doorways panels must pass through, and leave working room on two sides for assembly.
