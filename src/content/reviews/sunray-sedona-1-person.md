The [SunRay Sedona 1-Person (HL100K)](amazon:sunray-sedona-1-person) is the cedar outlier in a hemlock-dominated guide: a 36"W x 42"D x 75"H single-person cabin built from Canadian red cedar, with 5 carbon-nano heaters, chromotherapy, Bluetooth, and a 7-year structural warranty. Street pricing runs ~$1,799–$2,799 — premium for a 1-person unit, but cedar commands it. Check the current price on Amazon.

## Key specs

- Exterior dimensions: 36"W x 42"D x 75"H
- 5 carbon-nano far-infrared heaters
- 1290W, 120V/15A plug-and-play
- Canadian red cedar construction
- Chromotherapy + Bluetooth
- 7-year structural warranty

## Who it's for

Solo sauna users who want cedar. If the classic sauna scent matters to you — that warm cedar aroma that defines the traditional sauna experience for many people — the Sedona is the only model in this guide that delivers it. It suits buyers who see the sauna as a sensory ritual, not just heat therapy, and who are willing to pay the cedar premium.

## What stands out

**The wood.** Canadian red cedar is the story. We compared specs across this guide and every other cabin uses hemlock. Cedar is naturally more resistant to moisture and decay, darker and richer looking, and unmistakably aromatic. Owner reports on cedar cabins consistently mention the scent as a highlight — hemlock simply can't match it.

**Heaters.** Five carbon-nano heaters for a single-person cabin is generous coverage — effectively one heater per surface around the seated user, which should mean very even warmth with no cold spots.

**Structural warranty.** The 7-year structural warranty is the longest structural coverage in this guide, and it makes sense paired with cedar: the wood's natural durability is the reason manufacturers can back the structure that long.

**Features.** Chromotherapy and Bluetooth speakers round out a feature set that matches more expensive cabins, in a solo footprint. The chromotherapy lighting in particular suits the solo ritual — a quiet, personalized session environment that owners of lit cabins consistently mention as a highlight.

**Efficiency.** At 1290W, the Sedona draws less than most 2-person cabins, so per-session electricity stays modest — see our [running-costs guide](/posts/infrared-sauna-running-costs/) for the per-session math. The smaller interior also means quicker heat-up than larger cabins, so you're not waiting long to start.

## Considerations

Price is the obvious one: ~$1,799–$2,799 for a 1-person cabin is steep next to the Dynamic Elite at roughly half that. You're paying for cedar and the structural warranty — make sure you value both. Buyers who don't care about the cedar scent get the same solo sauna experience from the Elite for much less.

The footprint is also larger than the Elite's, so this isn't the pick for the tightest spaces — at 36"W x 42"D it needs a real corner, not a closet nook. One-person means one-person, always: if there's any chance of shared sessions, the 2-person models are the honest choice.

## Verdict

The SunRay Sedona 1-Person is the cedar choice: the only Canadian red cedar cabin in this guide, with generous heater coverage, chromotherapy, Bluetooth, and a 7-year structural warranty. It's priced like a premium solo unit because it is one. If the cedar experience is what you're after, nothing else here offers it — check the current price on Amazon.
